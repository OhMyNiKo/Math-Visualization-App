Field Explorer

Place charges and the electric field appears; nothing here is drawn by hand, every arrow is computed from Coulomb's law. Then release a test particle and watch it refuse to follow the field line.

The defining equation \(\displaystyle \mathbf E(\mathbf r)=\sum_i \frac{k\,q_i}{r_i^{2}}\,\hat{\mathbf r}_i \)  ·  \(\displaystyle \mathbf F=q\mathbf E=m\mathbf a \)  (k=1, q=+1, m=1)

Controls

Click empty space: add the selected charge. Drag a charge to move it. Double-click a charge to remove it. The white + disc is the test particle (positive, like a + charge, just white): drag it to choose the release point.

The misconception this kills

Field lines are not the paths particles follow. A field line shows the direction of force at each point; a moving particle keeps its momentum, so once the line curves, the particle leaves it. Release the particle: the dashed curve is the field line through the release point, the solid curve is where the particle actually goes. They separate by ? px after release. Only when lines are straight do the two agree.
