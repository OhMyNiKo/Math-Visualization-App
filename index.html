PHYSICS INTUITION APP · TWENTY-NINE LIVE MODULES

Drag it. Change it. Break it until it clicks.

Every module takes one hard concept, hands you the controls, and lets the mathematics answer in real time. Nothing on any screen is drawn by hand; move a slider and the physics recomputes. Each module exists to kill exactly one misconception, and each carries its defining equation on the panel. Fifteen physics modules and fourteen mathematics modules built for the CIE / A-Level classroom; the switch in the top bar flips every page between the dark and the light look, and each "Solved live" card has its own on/off so a teacher can hide the answers until the class has guessed.
