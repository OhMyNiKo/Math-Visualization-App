The misconception this kills

"A rose r = a cos kθ has k petals" is only half true: k odd gives k petals (the curve closes after θ = π), k even gives 2k petals, because the negative-r half of each turn lands on new ground instead of retracing. And negative r is not "nothing to draw": (−r, θ) is a real point, |r| from the pole on the opposite side, which is where the limaçon's inner loop and half of every rose come from. Right now: ?.
