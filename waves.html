The misconception this kills

Adding more waves does not smooth the overshoot away. On the square preset the sum spikes to ? beside every jump, an overshoot of about 9 percent of the jump size, and adding harmonics only makes the spike narrower, never shorter. That stubborn 9 percent is the Gibbs phenomenon: a perfectly sharp corner genuinely needs infinitely many smooth waves.
