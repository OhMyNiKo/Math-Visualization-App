The misconception this kills

"Changing variables just renames dx dy as du dv." It does not: a small du × dv cell is sent to a bent patch whose area is |det J| du dv, and that factor is what keeps ∬ f dA honest. The famous r in r dr dθ is not a convention or a decoration; it is exactly det J of the polar map, and it goes to zero at the pole because the map squashes a whole edge of the (r, θ) rectangle into one point.
