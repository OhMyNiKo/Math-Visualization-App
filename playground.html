Energy ladder (Eₙ ∝ n²/L²)

Change one parameter

Energy jumps as n²: the gap from n=1 to 2 is 3 units of E₁, from 2 to 3 it is 5, from 3 to 4 it is 7. And shrink L: every level climbs as 1/L², because squeezing a wave into less room forces a shorter wavelength, and shorter wavelength is higher energy. Confinement costs energy; that is why electrons do not fall into the nucleus. Interior nodes: always n−1 (count them: 0).
