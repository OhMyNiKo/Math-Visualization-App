The Integral Machine

Type a curve, drag the two amber walls to choose a region, and the machine slices it into rectangles. The exact area and the rectangle total are both printed live: now slide N and watch one number hunt down the other.

The misconception this kills

Integration is not an area formula that exists by magic; the integral is the limit of these rectangle totals. Right now the gap is ?; double N and it falls to roughly half (midpoint rule: a quarter). Push N to 300 and the two numbers become one. Every ∫ you will ever write is shorthand for exactly this stampede of rectangles.

Mathematics: exact value by composite Simpson on 4,000 subintervals (error far below the last printed digit for these smooth functions); rectangle totals by left, midpoint, or right rule.
