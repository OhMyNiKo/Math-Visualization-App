Arithmetic and Geometric Sequences

Set the first term and the step (a common difference or a common ratio), then watch the terms uₖ scatter on the left while the partial sums Sₖ pile up bar by bar on the right. For a geometric sequence with |r| < 1 the bars creep towards the limit a/(1−r) and the gap to it is drawn live; for |r| ≥ 1 they run away.

The misconception this kills

"Adding infinitely many positive numbers must give infinity" is false. When the terms shrink geometrically the sum closes in on a finite limit: each new term fills a fixed fraction of what is left. Right now the gap between Sₙ and the limit is ?.
