The misconception this kills

"More terms always make it better" is false. A Taylor polynomial is the best local impostor, unbeatable near a and possibly nonsense far away. Load ln(1+x), crank n up, and watch the fit improve inside |x−a| < 1 while it gets worse beyond: right now the probe error is ?. Convergence has a radius, and stepping outside it is how perturbation theory quietly lies to physicists.

Mathematics: coefficients computed by true Taylor-mode automatic differentiation (power-series arithmetic for + − × ÷ ^, exp, ln, sin/cos, tan, atan, sinh/cosh/tanh, up to order 10), not by finite differences; Tₙ evaluated by Horner in (x−a).
