The misconception this kills

Energy is never "used up". The ball slows, but the total ? does not move: with friction on, the missing motion is sitting in the heat account, to the last decimal. Twenty-eight blocks, always twenty-eight; friction only moves them to a ledger you cannot see by eye. That bookkeeping identity is what "conservation of energy" means, and Noether's theorem says it holds because physics today is the same as physics tomorrow.
