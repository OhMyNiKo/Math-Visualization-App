Phase Space

On the left, a pendulum or a spring you can grab. On the right, the same motion seen the way physicists actually see it: one point tracing a curve in the plane of position and momentum. Drag the bob anywhere, let go, and watch the whole history become geometry.

The defining equation \(\displaystyle \ddot\theta=-\frac{g}{\ell}\sin\theta-\gamma\dot\theta \)  · state = \(\displaystyle (\theta,\dot\theta) \), one point; motion = one curve

The misconception this kills

Damping does not just "make the swing smaller". In phase space the entire story is one curve: a closed loop when energy is conserved, and with air resistance on, a spiral falling into the resting point, E = ? and shrinking. Trajectories never cross themselves or each other; that single fact is why knowing (position, velocity) once determines the motion forever.

Physics: pendulum θ̈ = −(g/ℓ)sinθ − γθ̇ with g/ℓ = 9.8; spring ẍ = −(k/m)x − γẋ with k/m = 4; classic RK4 at dt = 1/240 with 4 substeps per frame. Faint curves in the phase plane are constant-energy contours; the separatrix (the contour through the inverted position) is drawn brighter.
