The ε–N Definition of a Limit

Pick a sequence and a candidate limit L, then squeeze the band L ± ε with the slider (or drag its edge on the canvas). The module hunts for the least N after which every term stays inside the band, and colours the terms that break out. Press Replay to let an adversary halve ε again and again and watch N answer each time.

The definition \(\displaystyle \lim_{n\to\infty} u_n = L \iff \forall \varepsilon>0\ \exists N:\ n>N \Rightarrow |u_n - L|<\varepsilon \) \(\displaystyle u_n = \tfrac{1}{n} \)

The misconception this kills

"The terms get closer and closer to L, so L is the limit" is not enough. Closer is cheap: (−1)ⁿ gets no closer to anything, and 1 + sin(n)/n wobbles yet converges.
